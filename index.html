1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Islam Everywhere (package com.islamofficialeverywhere.app), referred to as “the app,” “we,” or “us.” It explains how information is handled when you use the app.

The app is designed primarily to work locally. It does not require an account and does not upload your personal prayer history to a server operated by us.

2. Information kept on your device

The app may save the following information in local app storage:

  • Your optional display name, date of birth and selected prayer-calculation start age.
  • Your daily Salah status, Qaza records, totals, streaks and progress.
  • Your selected madhab, language, theme, reminder, sound and other settings.
  • Your premium-entitlement status and temporary rewarded-ad theme access.
  • Your last successfully obtained coordinates and calculated prayer schedule, so prayer times and Qibla can continue to work offline.

We do not receive this locally stored information. Android backup is disabled for the app. You can erase it using Reset all data in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Location, prayer times and Qibla

If you grant permission, the app accesses approximate or precise device location to calculate prayer times, Qibla direction and distance to Makkah. These calculations happen on your device. The most recent coordinates are cached only in local app storage for offline use; they are not uploaded to a server operated by us.

Location permission is optional. If declined, location-dependent features may be unavailable or may continue using a previously cached location.

Nearby Masjids

If you deliberately tap the option to find nearby masjids, the app opens a mapping app or Google Maps search and may include your coordinates in that request. The selected mapping provider then handles the request under its own privacy policy. The app does not send coordinates to a mapping provider unless you choose this action.

Google processes information under its own documentation. See the Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase, and Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Sharing, selling and disclosure

  • We do not sell your prayer records, profile information or cached location.
  • We do not share that local information with advertisers.
  • Google processes the service information described above for advertising, analytics, diagnostics, configuration and billing.
  • A mapping provider receives a nearby-masjid search only when you choose to open that external feature.
  • We may disclose information if legally required or necessary to protect users, rights and app security, but we cannot disclose local records that we do not possess.

If you contact us by email, your address, message and anything you voluntarily include are received by us and the email providers involved. We use them only to reply, provide support, investigate issues and maintain necessary correspondence.

7. Retention, security and international processing

Local information remains on your device until you reset it, clear app storage or uninstall the app. We do not operate cloud backup for prayer records. Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to address the request, maintain records and meet legal obligations.

Google retains service information according to its own policies and settings. Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than where you live, subject to their legal safeguards.

We use measures appropriate to a local-first app, including disabling Android backup for app data. No method of storage or electronic processing is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Location: deny or revoke permission in Android Settings. Clear local app data to remove cached coordinates.
  • Notifications and alarms: disable reminders in the app or change notification and exact-alarm access in Android Settings.
  • Advertising: use Ad privacy choices in Settings when available, and reset or delete your Advertising ID through Android privacy settings.
  • Local records: use Reset all data or uninstall the app.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel through Google Play. Access normally continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Support correspondence: contact us to request deletion of information you previously sent directly to us, subject to legal retention needs.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent. Because most app information is stored only on your device, the most direct way to exercise those rights for local records is through the controls above. You may contact us about information we actually possess.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child sent personal information through support email, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of the app and device permissions.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, service providers or legal requirements change. The revised page will show a new effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or store listing when appropriate.
